BlackRock Bitcoin ETF recorded strong institutional inflows this quarter.

Revenue from crypto-related financial products increased 18% year-over-year.
Operating margin improved from 21% to 28%.

Management stated that demand from institutional investors remains strong despite short-term volatility.

The company plans to expand digital asset exposure through additional ETF offerings in Asia during Q3.

Analysts noted:
- increasing trading volume
- stronger long-term adoption signals
- improving liquidity conditions

Risk factors:
- possible SEC regulatory tightening
- short-term macroeconomic uncertainty

Overall management tone during earnings call remained optimistic.
